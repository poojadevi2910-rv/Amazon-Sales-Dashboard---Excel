# Amazon Great Indian Festival Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive E-Commerce Data Analytics Dashboard built in Excel to analyze sales metrics, customer savings, order fulfillment speeds, regional performance, and payment methods during the Amazon Great Indian Festival event.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

![Amazon Great Indian Festival Dashboard](./Amazon_sales.jpg)

---

## 🚀 Key Business Metrics (KPIs)

* **Total GMV (Gross Merchandise Value):** ₹3,902 M
* **Savings Distributed:** ₹432 M
* **Total Orders Placed:** 78,650.00
* **Prime Member Orders:** 53,524.00 (~68% of total volume)
* **On-Time Deliveries:** 74,177.00 (~94.3% on-time fulfillment)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

### 1. Order Fulfillment & Delivery Speed
* **Dominant Fulfillment Mode:** Same-Day Delivery accounts for the highest share of total orders (32,238) followed by Next-Day Delivery (25,566) and Expedited Shipping (13,102).
* **Delivery Reliability:** Over 94% of total orders are delivered on-time (74,177 out of 78,650).

### 2. Category Performance by Membership Type
* Across all major categories (*Appliances, Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Smartphones*), **Prime Members** consistently represent **67%–69%** of sales volume, with **Smartphones** and **Home & Kitchen** leading in Prime adoption.

### 3. Payment Methods & Bank Discounts
* **Most Frequent Payment Methods:** 
  * **UPI:** 22.12% share of total transactions
  * **SBI Credit Card:** 18.05%
  * **SBI Debit Card:** 14.13%
  * **Amazon Pay Balance:** 12.76%
* **Promotional Value Delivered:** SBI Credit Cards drove the highest bank promotional discount value (₹3,54,61,883), followed by SBI Debit Cards (₹2,78,38,086) and UPI (₹1,73,35,765).

### 4. Order Status Distribution
* **Delivered:** 74,171 orders (Overwhelming majority)
* **In-Transit:** 2,863 orders
* **Returned:** 1,296 orders
* **Shipped:** 320 orders

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

* **Platform:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Formulas, Dynamic Charts, Slicers)
* **Data Visualizations:** KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Regional Map Chart, Stacked Column Chart, Horizontal Bar Chart, Clustered Column Chart
